# Draft email to Bloom (support@trybloom.ai) — decision-gating questions

**Subject:** Agency/client commercial + data terms at volume — evaluating Bloom for adoption

Hi Bloom team,

We're Trusted Marketing, a growth agency evaluating Bloom as our **primary** ad-creative
generation engine across our client portfolio (~13 brands, weekly test cadence, roughly
1,500–1,800 assets/month). The Claude Code MCP + learn-from-URL model is a great fit. Before we
adopt for client work, we need written confirmation on a few commercial and data points.

**Commercial / usage rights**
1. Can we use Bloom to generate creative for our **clients'** brands (not our own), run those as
   paid ads on the clients' behalf, and deliver/sublicense the resulting assets to those clients?
   Any restriction on agency, commercial, or resale use?
2. Your ToS says we retain ownership of generated assets — does that ownership extend to using
   them in paid advertising and handing them to clients with no further license from Bloom?

**Data / privacy** (gating for us — these are client brand assets)
3. Are uploaded or learned brand assets (logos, colors, fonts, reference images) used to **train**
   Bloom's or any third party's models? If so, is there an opt-out, and can training be
   contractually **disabled** for our account?
4. Do you offer a **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** and a current **sub-processor list**?
5. Are one account's brand profiles and generated assets **isolated** from other Bloom users? Your
   ToS notes "similar outputs may be generated for other users" — does that mean assets built on our
   clients' specific brand inputs could surface for third parties?

**IP**
6. Do you offer any IP/trademark **indemnification** for generated outputs, or is it entirely "as-is"?

**Portability (no vendor lock-in)**
7. Can we **bulk-export** all generated assets and brand profiles via the API at any time? On
   cancellation, is there an export window before deletion?

**Volume + technical**
8. What's your **custom/volume pricing** at ~1,500–1,800 assets/month across 13 brands? How does the
   credit model work per creative across sizes — does one generation yield multiple aspect ratios, and
   is `bloom_resize_image` billed separately?
9. Please confirm supported ad aspect ratios include **1:1, 4:5, and 9:16**.
10. API/MCP **rate limits, batch limits**, and any **uptime/SLA** for programmatic volume use.

Quick answers here unblock our evaluation — happy to hop on a call if that's easier.

Thanks,
Tom · Trusted Marketing
